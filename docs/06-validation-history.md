## Parser validation follow-up

Checks involving dates and times fix timeZone and use fixed time inputs. They do not call the current time at runtime (`Date.now` or `currentTime`). Native ICU support varies by OS and browser, so parity is meaningful only under the same Intl data and runtime matrix.

Identical argument schemas were shared within a parse call, and successful validation results for immutable translation and base-contract pairs were reused in a local implementation. In 20 cold 10k cross-comparisons, 17 were faster and the per-pair median difference was -36.08 ms, but p95 increased from 396.18 to 563.63 ms. Argument freezing in repeated fixtures fell to twice per call, and the message-freeze path was the next candidate. The 163 workspace tests and consumer type checks passed. End-to-end latency was not remeasured; conditions and raw results are in [18-shared-argument-schemas.md](18-shared-argument-schemas.md).

Structure-specific freezing paths were locally applied to parser-owned literal and argument ASTs. In 10k cold cross-comparisons, p50 was observed at 135.39→107.54 ms and p95 at 356.05→279.60 ms, and 15 of 20 pairs were faster. The median difference across 100 incremental pairs was +1.17 ms, so no improvement in ordinary edits was confirmed. The 164 workspace tests and consumer type checks passed.
